# Data check

Use this when someone asks whether they can trust a number, how something is calculated, or why a metric looks surprising or contradicts what they see on the board.

Start with a verdict, Strong, Directional or Weak. When the report includes dataChecks, use its verdict and findings as your starting point, since the person sees the same verdict in the briefing; you can add a reason it does not cover, but do not contradict it without saying why. Give the one or two reasons that matter most for this question, from: the size of the sample, which stages and item types are measured, the reporting window, whether the board history covers the period, and whether a percentile is being read more precisely than the sample allows. Explain the calculation only when they ask how it works.

When the question is only how a number is calculated, skip the verdict and answer with the calculation line.

People often mix up three different things; keep them apart in the answer. A calculation question is about how Steerloom computes the number. A source-data issue means the board history is incomplete, the workflow changed, or items were moved in bulk. A process signal is a real change in how work flows. Only the last one is something for the team to act on.

Steerloom's numbers come deterministically from the board history, so explain them rather than recomputing them from ticket text. When a check could change the verdict, name the smallest one, such as widening the reporting window or measuring another stage, and say what result would change your view.

# Making landing pages bolder

## Diagnose first

Flag these "conservative" symptoms:

- Text-only grids where every item has equal weight
- No numbers, visuals or product glimpses
- Same background for consecutive sections
- Headings that label ("What it measures") instead of promise
- CTA that's no louder than the rest of the page

## Fixes, in priority order

1. **Show, don't define.** Every feature gets a mini visual or a real example value. Definitions move to small text or hover.
2. **One hero per section.** Pick the most impressive item and make it 2x bigger (bento grid). Equal-sized grids are boring.
3. **Numbers as headlines.** Big example figures ("4.2 days", "85% by Nov 14") beat descriptions.
4. **Section rhythm.** Alternate light/dark or plain/gradient backgrounds. Use a dark section for trust/security content.
5. **Loud CTA.** Most saturated color on the page, biggest button, a peek of the actual product output, and a trust line (no credit card, read-only, etc.).
6. **Type contrast.** Headings at least 2.5x body size; tight letter-spacing on headings; lighter, muted body text.

## Copy rules

- Headings promise an outcome or take a stance: "Six numbers that tell the truth about your board" not "What it measures".
- Use contrast phrasing: "X, not Y" ("from when work moved, not when someone updated a ticket").
- Short, confident fragments for trust points: "Read-only. Always."
- Buttons describe the user's action: "Connect my board" not "Get started".
- Always provide a before/after table of copy changes.

## Workflow

1. List the conservative symptoms you see.
2. Propose a bolder layout per section (with the hero element named).
3. Rewrite copy.
4. Implement, with motion following the motion-landing skill. Be bold; the user prefers toning down over pushing up.

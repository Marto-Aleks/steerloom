Steerloom · About

What Steerloom is

Steerloom reads your team's Jira, Trello or Azure DevOps board and writes the briefing a delivery lead needs: what is stuck, why, what to raise today, and when the current work will actually finish. It is read-only, and it explains every number in plain words.

Who it is for

Delivery leads, engineering managers, agile coaches and project managers: the people who get asked "when will it be done?" and have to answer from a board that was not built to tell them.

What you get

A headline
How many active items have taken longer than usual, oldest first. "Usual" is your team's own history (the 85th percentile of recently finished items), never an industry number.
Decisions for today
Work piling up in one stage, items that came back into the flow, fewer items finishing than before. One row per signal, with the items.
A finish forecast
When the current work could be done at 50, 70, 85 and 95% confidence, from 10,000 replays of your own recent throughput.
The trend
Throughput, cycle time and the usual limit, each against the previous period, with the items behind every number.
How it's calculated
Behind every number: what it means, how it was read off your items, which items set it, and what was left out and why.
Steerloom AI
With your own key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok or a company gateway) it reads the tickets to say why work is stuck and who it waits on, and drafts talking points. It reads; it never writes to your board.

What it is not

Not another metrics dashboard. Tools such as ActionableAgile, LinearB or Swarmia show charts of cycle time, aging work and forecasts. Steerloom starts from the decision: what to raise today, why, and the evidence a team can check. It works from the board alone, with no git or IDE telemetry, and it never changes anything in your tracker.

Where your data lives

On steerloom.app, board history is read into memory for your session and not stored. The installed local version keeps everything on your computer and reaches company Jira servers behind a VPN that the hosted version cannot. Details in the privacy notice.
